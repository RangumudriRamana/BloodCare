FAQs for Blood Donation in India

1. Minimum Age Requirement
In India, the minimum age for blood donation is 18 years to ensure physical and mental maturity.

2. Minimum Weight
Donors must weigh at least 45 kg to safely tolerate blood collection.

3. Hemoglobin Requirement
Minimum hemoglobin should be 12.5 gm/100ml to prevent complications like anemia.

4. Who Can Donate?
Healthy individuals aged 18–65 years, weighing above 45 kg. First-time donors must be below 60 years.

5. Anemia & Underweight
Individuals with anemia or underweight should improve health before donating.

6. Safety of Donation
All blood is screened for HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis, and Malaria.

Diet Before Donation
Eat iron-rich foods like spinach, lentils, fish, and meat. Stay hydrated.

Before Donation Day
Sleep well, avoid alcohol, and eat a proper meal.

Exercise
Light exercise is fine, avoid overexertion.

How Much Blood Is Taken?
350–450 ml (less than 10% of total blood volume).

Duration
5–10 minutes for donation. Total process 30–60 minutes.

Platelet Donation
Done through apheresis. Takes longer but helps critical patients.

Lab Tests Performed
Blood typing, HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis, Malaria screening.

Storage Duration
Whole blood: 35–42 days. Components vary.

Side Effects
Mild dizziness or fatigue may occur but is temporary.

3-Month Interval Rule
Ensures complete recovery of blood cells.

Does Blood Group Change?
No, blood group remains constant for life.
